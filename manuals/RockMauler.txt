SECTION 1 — SAFETY & COMPLIANCE

1.1 General Safety

Only certified mining/construction personnel may operate or service the 

CR‐220.

Always perform Lockout/Tagout before accessing the crusher chamber or rotor 

assembly.

Maintain a minimum 4 m clearance around the feed hopper during operation.

Wear Class‐IV PPE: reinforced gloves, dust respirator, anti‐vibration boots.

1.2 Hazard Zones

Red Zone: Crusher chamber (crush hazard, high‐impact surfaces).

Orange Zone: Rotor assembly (entanglement hazard).

Yellow Zone: Feed hopper (fall‐in hazard).

1.3 AI‐Assisted Safety

The AI Field Engineer Tool automatically:

Detects chamber pressure spikes indicating imminent jams.

Predicts rotor overload conditions using torque harmonics.

Flags unsafe feed patterns (oversized rocks, uneven loading).

SECTION 2 — SYSTEM OVERVIEW

2.1 Machine Description

The RockMauler™ CR‐220 is a mid‐capacity excavation crusher designed for breaking 

down rock, ore, and construction debris directly at the job site. Its dual‐jaw system and 

rotor assembly provide high throughput with minimal downtime.

2.2 Core Subsystems

Crushing System: Dual jaw plates + RA‐220 rotor

Powertrain: MU‐18 motor + BD‐Flex belt drive

Control System: Shock‐resistant operator console

Sensor Array: Chamber pressure, rotor speed, jam detection

SECTION 4 — OPERATING PROCEDURES

4.1 Startup Sequence

1. Inspect feed hopper for debris.

2. Power on MU‐18 motor unit.

3. Run AI‐Assisted Rotor Calibration (8 seconds).

4. Activate belt drive system.

5. Begin controlled feeding of material.

4.2 Crushing Workflow

Maintain rotor speed between 1,200–1,450 RPM.

AI flags oversized material before chamber entry.

Use Vibro‐Assist discharge mode for wet or clay‐heavy loads.

SECTION 5 — FIELD DIAGNOSTICS

5.1 Common Failures

Chamber jams

Rotor torque overload

Belt slippage

Jaw plate misalignment

5.2 AI‐Assisted Diagnostics

The AI tool provides:

Chamber pressure spike graphs

Rotor torque harmonic analysis

Belt tension prediction

Jaw alignment deviation mapping

5.3 Field Repair Procedure: Clearing a Chamber Jam

1. Activate AI Jam Scan.

2. AI identifies jam type (material blockage, misaligned jaw, rotor obstruction).

3. Engage Jam‐Release Actuator (JRA‐10).

4. Remove obstructing material manually if safe.

5. Inspect jaw plates for wear >35%.

6. Re‐run jam scan to confirm chamber clearance.

SECTION 6 — MAINTENANCE

6.1 Daily Maintenance

6.2 Weekly Maintenance
