SECTION 1 — SAFETY & COMPLIANCE

1.1 General Safety

Only certified field engineers may operate or service the MB‐4400.

Always perform Lockout/Tagout before accessing hydraulic or drill components.

Maintain a minimum 5 m clearance around the drill mast during operation.

Wear Class‐IV mining PPE: respirator, vibration‐rated gloves, reinforced boots.

1.2 Hazard Zones

Red Zone: Active drilling radius (high‐velocity debris).

Orange Zone: Hydraulic line cluster (burst risk).

Yellow Zone: Stabilizer outriggers (crush hazard).

1.3 AI‐Assisted Safety

The AI Field Engineer Tool automatically:

Flags unstable ground conditions using vibration mapping.

Predicts mast oscillation hazards.

Issues thermal overload warnings for hydraulic components.

SECTION 2 — SYSTEM OVERVIEW

2.1 Machine Description

The TerraBore™ MB‐4400 is a high‐precision blast‐drill rig designed for deep mining 

operations requiring accurate bore placement, high torque output, and stable drilling on 

uneven terrain.

2.2 Core Subsystems

Drill System: BoreHead BH‐2200 rotary assembly

Hydraulic System: HP‐900 high‐pressure pump

Control System: Ruggedized operator console

Sensor Array: Oscillation, depth, thermal, torque

SECTION 4 — OPERATING PROCEDURES

4.1 Startup Sequence

1. Engage stabilizer outriggers.

2. Power on the control console.

3. Run Self‐Calibration Cycle (12 seconds).

4. Select drill bit size.

5. Initiate Ground Stability Scan (AI‐assisted).

6. Begin drilling.

4.2 Drilling Workflow

Maintain torque within 70–85% of rated load.

AI flags deviations above 0.8 mm oscillation.

Use dust suppression cartridges for deep bores.

SECTION 5 — FIELD DIAGNOSTICS

5.1 Common Failures

Mast oscillation

Torque instability

Hydraulic pressure drop

Depth sensor drift

5.2 AI‐Assisted Diagnostics

The AI tool provides:

Oscillation heatmaps

Torque waveform analysis

Hydraulic leak prediction

Depth sensor recalibration

5.3 Field Repair Procedure: Stabilizing Mast Oscillation

1. Activate AI Oscillation Scan.

2. AI identifies oscillation source (mast segment, couplings, ground instability).

3. Tighten mast segment clamps to AI‐recommended torque values.

4. Replace dampening rings if wear exceeds 40%.

5. Re‐run oscillation scan to confirm stability.
