SECTION 1 — SAFETY & COMPLIANCE

1.1 General Safety

Only certified underground‐operations personnel may operate or service the 

DV‐3100.

Perform Lockout/Tagout before accessing suspension, drivetrain, or load‐bed 

components.

Maintain a minimum 5 m clearance behind the haul truck during reversing 

operations.

Wear Class‐V mining PPE: respirator, reinforced boots, high‐visibility 

underground gear.

1.2 Hazard Zones

Red Zone: Rear haul bed (crush hazard during dumping).

Orange Zone: Suspension cluster (high‐compression hazard).

Yellow Zone: Engine compartment (thermal and fume hazard).

1.3 AI‐Assisted Safety

The AI Field Engineer Tool automatically:

Predicts suspension imbalance using terrain‐response analytics.

Detects load‐weight anomalies that may cause rollover risk.

Flags drivetrain overheating conditions before critical thresholds.

SECTION 2 — SYSTEM OVERVIEW

2.1 Machine Description

The DeepMiner™ DV‐3100 is a heavy‐duty subsurface haul truck engineered for 

transporting ore, debris, and structural materials through narrow underground corridors. 

Its terrain‐adaptive suspension and high‐traction drivetrain ensure stability on uneven 

mine floors.

2.2 Core Subsystems

Suspension System: QS‐3100 dampers + load‐balancing struts

Powertrain: TD‐900 TurboDiesel engine + TR‐MegaTorque transmission

Load System: Reinforced haul bed with RockShield coating

Sensor Array: Suspension imbalance, terrain mapping, load weight

SECTION 4 — OPERATING PROCEDURES

4.1 Startup Sequence

1. Inspect haul bed for structural cracks.

2. Power on TD‐900 engine.

3. Run AI‐Assisted Suspension Calibration (12 seconds).

4. Engage drivetrain.

5. Begin controlled haul operations.

4.2 Hauling Workflow

Maintain load weight within 85% of rated capacity.

AI flags terrain instability before entering narrow corridors.

Use low‐gear mode for steep inclines or wet mine floors.

5.2 AI‐Assisted Diagnostics

Suspension imbalance heatmaps

Load distribution modeling

5.3 Field Repair Procedure: Correcting Suspension Imbalance

1. Activate AI Suspension Scan.

2. AI identifies imbalance source (QS‐3100 damper, strut, linkage, terrain 

mismatch).

3. Inspect QS‐3100 damper for compression deviation >18%.

4. Replace load‐balancing strut if structural wear exceeds 30%.

5. Realign terrain‐response linkages.

6. Re‐run suspension scan to confirm stability.
